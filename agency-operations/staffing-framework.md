# Boutique agency staffing: research and role framework

Research date: 8 November 2026. Status: proposed reference framework for review.

## Scope and limitations

Desk research of six agencies' own public career/team pages: five Egyptian examples and one UK hospitality/social specialist. This is not an interview survey, salary survey, or statistically representative benchmark. Job descriptions show advertised responsibilities, not verified filled posts, internal profitability, or universal client-to-staff ratios.

## Proposed lean role architecture

This is a consultant inference from the source patterns, not a copied industry-standard organization chart.

- Owners / portfolio leads: commercial direction, sales, contracts, strategic client relationships and final approval of major changes.
- Account & Delivery Manager: production queue, scope tracking, dates, client delivery communication, consolidated feedback, approved publishing and administrative invoice follow-up.
- Part-time Creative & Social Strategy Lead: audience/content strategy, monthly concepts, key briefs, brand standards and creative quality.
- Shared creative contributors: design, photography, video, mobile content and agreed post-production.
- Optional commissioned specialists: writing, editing, localization, paid media or accounting when actual scope requires them.

Each account has one accountable portfolio owner. One owner sponsors the delivery role and resolves conflicting priorities. The delivery manager controls the schedule; the creative lead controls creative standards within approved scope. Team members receive one consolidated task queue.

A part-time creative retainer must specify hours, account coverage, deliverables, review windows and exclusions. An advisory retainer alone will not transfer monthly planning from owners. Detailed writing, edits and production must have named capacity.

## Hiring sequence and validation

1. Inventory contractual deliverables and baseline owner/production time.
2. Establish delivery ownership and a single queue.
3. Trial senior creative support on representative accounts before widening coverage.
4. Measure planning, writing, design, capture, editing, revisions, publishing and reporting separately.
5. Add the specific missing capacity when sustained demand exceeds available hours.

Remote delivery management can work when local creators execute independently from approved briefs and asset handoffs are reliable. Do not use client count alone to determine staffing. Shoot supply and post-production supply must be measured separately.

Review success using on-time delivery, required approvals, substantive rework, owner time released and client feedback. Set targets after baseline measurement. Hiring cost and affordability require actual quotes and financial inputs; none are asserted here.

## Adoption boundaries

This file is a reusable, public-source framework. It does not publish named employee records, private account assessments, salaries, performance findings or client invoices. The detailed company adoption plan belongs in an appropriately private company workspace. Proposed roles are not approved appointments, job postings or commitments.
