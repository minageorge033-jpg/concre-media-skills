# Creative and campaign review

Evaluate against the supplied brief and actual artifact. Inspect rendered visual deliverables when tools permit; state what could not be inspected.

Apply the relevant lenses:

- Strategy: audience, business objective, offer, and desired action align.
- Message: hook, proposition, supporting proof, and CTA are understandable and consistent.
- Brand and craft: voice, hierarchy, typography, originality, legibility, and production quality serve the idea.
- Channel and audience: execution fits viewing context, language/dialect, accessibility, and verified technical requirements.
- Trust and readiness: claims have evidence; rights, dates, links, pricing, and approval status are known.
- Measurement: tracking and success criteria match the objective; analysis separates evidence from hypothesis.

For each finding, identify the affected asset or location, observed issue, practical consequence, and concrete correction. Prioritize launch blockers, material improvements, and optional polish. Avoid unsupported numerical scores.

Fix authorized issues and inspect the revision. Report remaining blockers and limitations. Review does not establish legal compliance, platform acceptance, or campaign effectiveness. An audit alone does not authorize publication.
